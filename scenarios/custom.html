Ideas to try

Berlin, 1750

Frederick the Great, Euler, the Academy of Sciences, and early Prussia.

Kyoto, 1600

The eve of the Tokugawa shogunate — Sekigahara, steelcraft, woodblock print, and strict hierarchy.

Babylon, 590 BC

Nebuchadnezzar II, astronomy, brick engineering, bronze and early iron.

Tenochtitlan, 1450

Floating chinampas, obsidian craftsmanship, causeways — no iron, no draft animals, 70 years before Cortés.

Alexandria, 250 BC

The Great Library, Eratosthenes measuring the Earth, Hero's predecessors, and Ptolemaic patronage.

Bell Labs, 1947

Months before the transistor — can you explain anything a room full of modern physicists doesn't already know?

How custom eras stay historically grounded

When you pick a place and year without a dedicated campaign file, your AI loads the complete 510-technology reference with hard prerequisites and first-appearance dates across 12 world regions. That prevents anachronisms (like paper in ancient Rome or printing in 1347 Florence) and lets craftsmen challenge every missing step realistically.
